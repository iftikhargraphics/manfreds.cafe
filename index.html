Our Story

A quiet corner that changes shape with the day.

Manfred's opened on a small corner of Rustlings Road with a simple idea — build one room that works as well at 8am as it does at 8pm. By day, that means a dialled-in espresso bar with a short, deliberate menu. By evening, the lights drop, the taps open, and the same room becomes a neighbourhood lagerbier bar.

We roast with a small Sheffield roastery, pour lagerbier from a handful of independent brewers, and bake with a bakery two streets away. Everything on the menu has a reason to be there — nothing is filler.
